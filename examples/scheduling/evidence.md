## Method

Recipe&#58; r&#45;schedule

Question&#58; When would a small team need more than a shared rota spreadsheet?

Compare a frontline employee satisfied with a stable rota and an approver handling frequent changes&#46; Explore rejection as well as adoption&#46;

### Dimensions

- role&#58; employee, approver
- schedule&#58; stable, changing

### Constraints

- Two contrasting roles, not all four combinations
- No browsing or real customer claims
- Include a role satisfied with the current alternative
